## Approved retention schedule

Ordinary retention periods are maximum windows, not minimum holds. A valid earlier deletion request overrides them, subject only to a specific justified exception. Expiry work may be operator-run for this small cohort; no automatic purge is claimed. Track due dates and review upcoming expiries weekly so deletion happens by each recorded expiry, rather than a later periodic sweep.

| Category | Approved window and trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Outpost account, enrollment and minimum reward-grant receipts | While actively used, then up to **12 months after the last player-initiated connected activity**. Automated settlement does not restart this clock. If the whole internal-test service retires, remove remaining records within **30 days of retirement**, unless the tester chooses migration to a successor test. The earlier applicable expiry controls. |
| Detailed Survey Works event data | Command history, checkpoints, request receipts, device-control history, scores and leaderboard aliases: **90 days after that event closes**, or earlier account deletion. Afterward, only the minimum reward receipt needed for the account follows the account window. Event closure is distinct from retiring the whole test service and does not immediately end cloud campaign recovery. |
| Cloud campaign backups | Keep the **latest successful and immediately previous** backup during the account recovery window. Remove superseded and orphaned uploads within **30 days**. Remove **all** uploaded versions and metadata on account expiry or fulfilled deletion. Include failed/conflicting uploads and files no longer referenced by either manifest. |
| Tau Ceti Labs-controlled diagnostic logs and exports | **30 days**. A relevant extract needed for a specific unresolved support issue follows the support-attachment schedule, rather than retaining entire logs indefinitely. |
| Support correspondence | Emails: **12 months after issue closure**. Uploaded saves and diagnostic attachments: remove within **90 days of closure**. A valid earlier deletion request overrides ordinary retention, subject to a specific justified exception. |
| Minimal deletion-completion record | **12 months after completion**: case number, dates, scope and outcome. Avoid keeping the full request, saves, account identifiers or email merely to prove completion. Do not label hashes of identifiers anonymous. |

No standing legal/security/dispute exception has been identified or approved. Any specific justified exception must have a documented purpose, minimal scope and expiry, with relevant details explained to the requester. Do not preserve a deleted tester's full event history, scores or backups merely for general research or speculative abuse prevention.

Provider-controlled records must be audited separately. [Unity Cloud Code logging](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/cloud-code/logging/overview) documents seven days for that logging service; this does not establish the retention of all Unity infrastructure records. Verify provider residual copies, archives and mailbox/provider retention before making broader deletion promises.

## Approved manual request procedure

1. **Intake and tracking.** Chris checks the confirmed mailbox each business day and designates a trusted backup for absences. Keep a private case register with original receipt time, scope, ownership-verification status, due dates and outcome; restrict access to the handler and approved backup. Use the minimal completion-record schedule after closure. Set retention/expiry dates for enrollment, events, backup files, diagnostic exports and closed support cases; review upcoming deadlines weekly.
2. **Acknowledge within three business days.** Supply a case number and clarify whole-account/associated-data deletion, backups-only or support-only. Explain that whole-account deletion removes connected event progress, leaderboard presence and cloud recovery. Full account deletion includes associated support correspondence under Tau Ceti Labs' control, subject to a specific justified exception and the minimal completion record.
3. **Verify using the established enrollment contact.** During enrollment, securely map the exact game-service account to the tester's verified existing contact. Independently confirm through that established channel, not a new address supplied only in the deletion request. An Engineer alias is only a lookup aid. No email scope is requested from Unity, so do not infer provider-email ownership from the request sender. Provide a safe manual lost-access/missing-mapping route without requiring app reinstallation. Never ask for passwords, provider sign-in codes, access tokens or raw credentials. Log the verification outcome/method without retaining unnecessary evidence copies.
4. **Use one repeatable administrator purge procedure.** Confirm scope and identity, stop new event writes and settlement for the account, and drain/cancel/coordinate already-running work. Remove enrollment/settlement references, protected profile/checkpoints/history/retry receipts/device controls, score/alias/metadata on every relevant leaderboard (including archives if present), reward records, **all** account-owned Cloud Save files and metadata, and related support/diagnostic records within scope. Delete the Outpost Authentication identity after related records have been handled. A list of intended targets before deletion and repeatable recovery from partial failure are required. Deleting Authentication alone is not proof that other service records were erased.
5. **Verify and control re-entry.** Read back every category and confirm absence. Verify that cached sessions, pending requests, retries and scheduled workers cannot recreate records after deletion. Fresh developer approval is required before the deleted participant re-enrolls. Define and test subsequent provider sign-in and local-save re-upload/restore behavior, including reward receipts. Outpost account deletion does not silently erase local campaign/practice files or delete the wider Unity Player Account.
6. **Complete and communicate.** Aim to complete within **30 calendar days of original receipt**, including ownership checks. Do not restart the clock after verification. If verification or provider handling remains unresolved, explain the status before that deadline; any shorter applicable legal deadline takes precedence. Send what was deleted, any specifically justified retained record and its expiry, and any separate provider action needed. Remove unnecessary request/verification copies and retain only the approved minimal completion record. The three-day acknowledgment and 30-day completion target are approved operating commitments, not a claim of a universal statutory deadline.
